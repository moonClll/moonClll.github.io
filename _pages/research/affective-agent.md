# A General-Purpose Agent for Affective Image Generation and Editing

*Research project · Media Computing Laboratory, Nankai University · March 2026 – July 2026*

![Agent-based affective image generation and editing](/images/affective-agent.png)

## Overview

This project explored agent-based approaches to affective image generation and editing, with an emphasis on prompt complexity, diffusion encoder configurations, and emotion-oriented reward strategies.

## My contribution

- Organized experimental datasets by aligning text prompts, target emotion labels, and generated images.
- Analyzed preliminary results under different prompt complexities and diffusion encoder configurations using emotion classification accuracy and Fréchet Inception Distance (FID).
- Evaluated reward strategies based on softmax emotion-classification scores and Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain (NDCG) for emotion ranking.
- Compared the two reward strategies to study their effects on emotional alignment and generation quality.

## Preliminary results

The NDCG-based strategy achieved **0.72** emotion classification accuracy and **30** FID, compared with **0.68** and **34**, respectively, for the softmax-based strategy.

# 通用型情感图像生成与编辑智能体

*研究项目 · 南开大学媒体计算实验室 · 2026 年 3 月 – 2026 年 7 月*

![基于智能体的情感图像生成与编辑](/images/affective-agent.png)

## 项目概述

本项目探索基于智能体的情感图像生成与编辑方法，重点关注提示词复杂度、扩散编码器配置和面向情绪的奖励策略。

## 我的贡献

- 整理实验数据集，对齐文本提示词、目标情绪标签和生成图像。
- 使用情绪分类准确率和 Fréchet Inception Distance（FID），分析不同提示词复杂度与扩散编码器配置下的初步结果。
- 使用基于 softmax 情绪分类分数和归一化折损累计增益（NDCG）的奖励策略进行情绪排序评估。
- 对比两种奖励策略，分析其对情绪一致性和生成质量的影响。

## 初步结果

基于 NDCG 的策略取得 **0.72** 的情绪分类准确率和 **30** 的 FID；基于 softmax 的策略对应结果为 **0.68** 和 **34**。
