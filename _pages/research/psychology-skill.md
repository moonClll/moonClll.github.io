# Psychology-Skill-Driven Continuous Visual Emotion Analysis

*CVPR 2027 submission · Media Computing Laboratory, Nankai University · July 2026 – Present*

![Psychology-skill-driven continuous visual emotion analysis]({{ '/images/psychology-skill.png' | relative_url }})

## Overview

This project investigates whether structured psychological knowledge can improve continuous visual emotion analysis by equipping multimodal large language models with reusable, psychology-informed reasoning skills.

## My contribution

- Designed and implemented psychology-based skills grounded in established psychological theories.
- Translated different theoretical perspectives into structured reasoning capabilities callable by multimodal large language models.
- Organized datasets and prepared experimental inputs with standardized data formats, labels, and evaluation inputs.
- Designed preliminary experiments covering psychological skills, model configurations, and reasoning strategies.
- Participated in main experiments across **nine continuous visual emotion analysis datasets** and analyzed the effect of psychology-based skills on continuous emotion prediction.

## Research status

CVPR 2027 submission.

# 心理学技能驱动的连续视觉情绪分析

*CVPR 2027 投稿 · 南开大学媒体计算实验室 · 2026 年 7 月 – 至今*

![心理学技能驱动的连续视觉情绪分析]({{ '/images/psychology-skill.png' | relative_url }})

## 项目概述

本项目研究结构化心理学知识能否通过为多模态大语言模型提供可复用的心理学推理技能，提升连续视觉情绪分析能力。

## 我的贡献

- 基于成熟的心理学理论设计并实现心理学技能。
- 将不同理论视角转化为多模态大语言模型可调用的结构化推理能力。
- 整理数据集并准备实验输入，统一数据格式、标签和评估输入。
- 设计初步实验，覆盖不同心理学技能、模型配置和推理策略。
- 在 **9 个连续视觉情绪分析数据集**上参与主要实验，并分析心理学技能对连续情绪预测的影响。

## 研究状态

CVPR 2027 投稿。
