# Synergistic Appearance–Geometry Reconstruction for Multi-Illumination Anomaly Detection

*IEEE CASE 2026 · Third author · September 2025 – March 2026*

![Synergistic appearance and geometry reconstruction for multi-illumination anomaly detection](/images/sagr-overview.png)

## Overview

This project investigated unsupervised industrial anomaly detection when illumination and geometric conditions vary. The method combines appearance and geometric information to improve anomaly localization on the M2AD benchmark.

## My contribution

- Reviewed work on unsupervised anomaly detection, multi-illumination modeling, photometric stereo, and cross-modal anomaly detection.
- Reproduced and evaluated CDO, RD++, MSFlow, Dinomaly, and INP-Former under a unified experimental protocol.
- Participated in training-parameter tuning, experimental design, comparative evaluation, and result analysis.
- Created visualizations of quantitative results and anomaly localization maps for the paper.

## Results

SAGR achieved **87.0% I-AUROC** and **92.6% AUPRO** on M2AD, improving upon Dinomaly by **4.0** and **6.7 percentage points**, respectively.

## Publication

Yuxin Zhang, Yuqi Cheng, **Yuhan Zhang**, Yunkang Cao, and Weiming Shen. “Synergistic Appearance–Geometry Reconstruction for Multi-Illumination Anomaly Detection.” *2026 IEEE 22nd International Conference on Automation Science and Engineering (IEEE CASE)*, 2026.

*Third author.*

# 面向多光照异常检测的外观–几何协同重建

*IEEE CASE 2026 · 第三作者 · 2025 年 9 月 – 2026 年 3 月*

![多光照异常检测的外观与几何协同重建](/images/sagr-overview.png)

## 项目概述

本项目研究光照和几何条件发生变化时的无监督工业异常检测问题，在 M2AD 基准上结合外观信息与几何信息以提升异常定位能力。

## 我的贡献

- 调研无监督异常检测、多光照建模、光度立体和跨模态异常检测等方向。
- 按照统一实验协议复现并评估 CDO、RD++、MSFlow、Dinomaly 和 INP-Former。
- 参与训练参数调优、实验设计、对比评估和结果分析。
- 为论文制作定量结果和异常定位图的可视化。

## 实验结果

SAGR 在 M2AD 上取得 **87.0% I-AUROC** 和 **92.6% AUPRO**，相较 Dinomaly 分别提升 **4.0** 和 **6.7 个百分点**。

## 论文信息

*第三作者。*
